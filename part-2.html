01Context

ZachXBT named the launderers. We followed the money.

On 24 Sep 2026 attackers drained Bitget's hot wallets. Bitget later raised the loss to $387.5M across Ethereum and EVM chains, XRP Ledger, TRON and Zcash. Bitget and TRM point to North Korea.

On 28 Sep ZachXBT published five aliases of Chinese launderers who openly asked THORChain-ecosystem support channels why their XRP→BTC swaps were stuck. The same day THORChain refused Bitget's request to block the attacker.

This update covers what wasn't public yet: where the money actually went, how the legs connect, and who got paid for washing it.

"Decentralization is a design principle, not a shield for facilitating known stolen funds." (Gracy Chen, Bitget CEO)
02The big one

A 0.6% "laundering fee", cashed out to OKX

Every THORChain swap memo can carry an affiliate: the interface that routed the swap and the fee it takes. Most affiliates are public apps. One wasn't.

thor18dvgrgpvxlh7rhhld4qjyrxs9c7tvwdakrkjm4 has no registered name. It appeared as the affiliate on 401 swaps, all on 26–27 Sep, 397 of them from XRP, charging up to 0.6%. 15 of its 16 senders are the Bitget attacker's XRP wallets. The 16th is the address itself, cashing out. It had no other customers. It looks like the launderers' own fee wallet.

Ethereum

11Method

How, and what we don't claim

Method
  • Ethereum: Blockscout / Etherscan labels; followed ETH and major tokens from labeled "Bitget Exploiter" wallets. Only wallets first seen after the hack count as attacker wallets. THORChain vaults and busy services were excluded.
  • XRP: full account_tx crawl from the Bitget XRP address; memos decoded from every THORChain deposit.
  • TRON: TronGrid; swap outputs read from USDT Transfer logs.
  • BTC: mempool.space; CoinJoin detected by structure (many equal outputs).
  • Affiliates: THORName registry + Midgard via public gateways.
Not claimed
  • Who owns the OKX account, or that it's the attacker rather than an intermediary.
  • That any interface, market maker, bridge or exchange acted knowingly.
  • Attribution to North Korea. That's Bitget's and TRM's assessment.
  • Identities behind Discord/Telegram aliases.
  • Final numbers. Funds were still moving at publication.
