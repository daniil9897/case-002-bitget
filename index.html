§ 03 · The case in 60 seconds

Six moves, from theft to cash-out

🔓
24 Sep · 18:31 UTC

The heist

Attackers compromise a backend system in Bitget's wallet infrastructure and spoof transaction data. The exchange signs off on its own robbery: $387.5M leaves across seven chains.

⏱️
19:08 → 19:29

21 minutes

$47.6M in USDT/USDC plus tokenized gold becomes 22,327 ETH through UniswapX before Tether or Circle can freeze it, via a wallet upgraded with EIP-7702.

Split, wait, drain

ETH sits in round 10,000-ETH wallets, then gets relayed into a "funnel" wallet and sprayed across 246 fresh addresses. XRP goes through 239 more. On 29 Sep the last one empties.

🌉

Bridge to Bitcoin

About $187M goes through THORChain, mostly into BTC, with extras via LayerZero, Skip:Go, Chainflip, Symbiosis and NEAR Intents. THORChain refuses Bitget's request to block the attacker.

💸

The launderers take a cut

A THORChain affiliate whose only customer is the attacker charges up to 0.6%, swaps the fees to USDT and deposits 131,982 USDT to an OKX account.

🇰🇵
Apr → Sep 2026

Same laundromat

The storefront "naswap" was registered 41 hours after the Kelp DAO hack. 99% of what it ever swapped comes from Kelp DAO, AFX Trade and Bitget, the first two already attributed to TraderTraitor.

§ 10 · Explainers

How crypto laundering actually works

The tools in this case, each in plain words and how it showed up here.

⚡THORChainSwap BTC for ETH with no account

A cross-chain exchange: you send coins to a vault with a short text memo, for example "=:BTC.BTC:bc1q…", and the network sends the other coin to that address. No sign-up, no KYC.

Here: ~$187M of Bitget funds went through it. The memos are public, and that's how we found every destination.

🏷️AffiliatesWho routed the swap, and their cut

Apps building on THORChain add their name and a fee in basis points to the memo. It's how wallets and interfaces get paid.

Here: two affiliates existed almost only for stolen money, naswap (Kelp, AFX, Bitget) and thor18dvg… (Bitget only), which cashed its fees to OKX.

🧊Why stablecoins get frozenand why this attacker moved fast

Tether and Circle can blacklist an address and make USDT/USDC on it unmovable. ETH, BTC and XRP have no such switch.

Here: the attacker swapped $47.6M of stablecoins in 21 minutes. Only ~$318K got frozen.

🌀CoinJoin (Wasabi)Mixing coins with strangers

Many users combine coins into one big transaction with equal-sized outputs, so it's hard to tell whose output is whose. Outputs often come in standard sizes like powers of two.

Here: ~$3.94M hit mixers. naswap's first funding came from inputs of 262,144 and 16,384 sats, typical CoinJoin denominations.

🏦Exchange deposit addressesThe weak spot of every launderer

Exchanges give each customer a personal deposit address that auto-forwards to a big "hot wallet". If you can see that forwarding, you've found an account the exchange can identify and freeze.

Here: 0x51B2…B7be forwards to "OKX: Hot Wallet 5" within minutes. That's how the OKX lead was found.

🧬EIP-7702A normal wallet with smart powers

A 2025 Ethereum upgrade lets an ordinary wallet temporarily behave like a smart contract, for example to batch approvals and swaps in one go.

Here: the attacker's swap wallet used MetaMask's StatelessDeleGator to dump $47.6M fast.

🎣Address poisoningScammers scamming the hacker

Bots send fake tokens from look-alike addresses (same first and last characters) and hope you copy the wrong one from your history.

Here: every exploiter wallet was spammed with fake "E឴឴ꓔH" and "Ú5឴឴DC" tokens within minutes.

🌉Bridges & chain-hoppingWhy money jumps across chains

Moving value between chains (TRON → Ethereum → Bitcoin) breaks simple tracking and lets launderers use whichever swap venue won't block them.

Here: TRX → USDT → USDT0 bridge → ETH → THORChain → BTC → Wasabi.

🕶️Zcash shielded poolWhere on-chain tracing goes dark

Zcash has transparent "t-addresses" (visible) and shielded addresses (encrypted). Once coins go shielded, amounts and destinations are hidden; analysts can only match amounts and timing coming out.

Here: 18,581 ZEC is still on a transparent address, and small chunks began moving on 29 Sep.
