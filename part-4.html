02Why bother

Why go back?

Breaking the trail

Every jump between chains and assets adds a step for an analyst to link. BTC → ETH → DAI → USDT → USDT0 → USDD crosses three chains and five assets in about 20 hours. Each swap goes through a different venue: THORChain, CoW Swap, Uniswap v4, LayerZero and SunSwap.

Picking what can't be frozen

USDT is used only as a bridge token, for about a minute at each end. The money rests in DAI on Ethereum and in USDD on TRON, stablecoins without an issuer freeze. This is the lesson from the first 21 minutes, when the attacker dumped USDT and USDC before Tether and Circle could act.

03Another naswap route

A Bitcoin hub mixing old and new

naswap kept selling the other direction too. 69 naswap swaps from Bitget laundering wallet 0x1cdac13e…ddb7 (29 Sep 06:51 → 30 Sep 08:14) all paid out to one Bitcoin address, 38mHJzN9v3b9brRsCMZexCbEsekcmR4rwi. Every one of the 14.55 BTC it ever received came from those swaps.

It forwards round amounts (1.0, 1.23, 1.67 BTC…) to bc1qcucpas769r9rfpah0fv89wtqnsjgl63keycaj7. That hub has received 88.3 BTC so far. In the same blocks, the hub is also fed by bc1qle6cq42zs5uur72hl2n5raghyyyux0c0g26edr, a wallet that already received THORChain payouts in April–May 2025 and that we flagged to Bitget on 28 Sep as pre-existing infrastructure. From the hub, BTC leaves in round lots to fresh addresses. Bitget's tracker logged its latest 2 BTC move at 01:33 UTC today.

06Method

How, and what we don't claim

Method
  • naswap swaps from THORChain Midgard (affiliate=naswap); BTC inputs and spends from mempool.space.
  • ETH and token flows from Blockscout; the poisoning spam (fake DAI/USDT/ETH tokens and look-alike senders) is excluded. Only the canonical DAI and USDT contracts are counted.
  • Bridge destinations decoded from USDT0 OFT send() calldata: dstEid 30420 (TRON), and the recipient bytes32 converted to TRON base58.
  • TRON transfers from TronGrid; ZEC balance from Blockchair; buckets from Bitget's public tracker.
Not claimed
  • Who controls any of these wallets. We name no person.
  • That CoW Swap, Uniswap, LayerZero/USDT0, SunSwap or THORChain acted knowingly.
  • That USDD can or cannot be frozen by any party. We report what Bitget's tracker states.
  • Exact amounts inside the Zcash shielded pool, which are hidden by design.
