01The finding

naswap: the swap storefront built for stolen money

In Part 2 we showed that every THORChain swap memo names an affiliate, the interface that routed it. One registered name kept showing up in the Bitget attacker's swaps: naswap. Across its whole history it routed 1,803 swaps from only 24 senders.

We classified every one of them. 1,787 swaps (99%) and $49.6M of $50.5M (98%) came from wallets belonging to three hacks. Two of them, Kelp DAO and AFX Trade, were publicly attributed to North Korea's TraderTraitor group before Bitget ever happened. The remaining 16 small swaps ($0.87M) include the operator's own test transactions.

06Off-chain

The laundromat has a storefront

naswap isn't only a name in swap memos. It is a live website, naswap.io, with a Chinese/English interface built on THORChain. It offers exactly three routes: ETH → BTC, XRP → BTC and XRP → ETH, the same routes the Bitget, Kelp DAO and AFX Trade funds took.

naswap.io · captured 30 Sep 2026 without interacting

What its own code says

  • AFFILIATE_NAME = 'naswap', the same THORName in the laundering memos
  • AFFILIATE_FEE_BPS = 30 (0.3%), the fee seen on most naswap swaps
  • A vip.naswap.io mirror where the fee is 0, which explains the zero-fee naswap swaps
  • An in-page XRP wallet unlock by seed phrase ("use only a dedicated small wallet") that lets one operator drive many XRP wallets
  • Libraries loaded via cdn.jsdmirror.com, a jsDelivr mirror used for access from mainland China; Chinese is the first language option
  • BUILD_TIME = '2026-08-31 16:00', 24 days before the Bitget hack

Born the same day as the on-chain name

Certificate Transparency logs show the first TLS certificates for naswap.io were issued on 20 Apr 2026, the same day the THORName "naswap" was registered, 41 hours after the Kelp DAO hack. The website and the on-chain affiliate were launched together. crt.sh ↗ · certificate log (JSON)

08Method

How, and what we don't claim

Method

  • Zcash via Blockchair; ETH via Blockscout / Etherscan; balances as of 29 Sep 18:54 UTC.
  • OFAC SDN digital-currency addresses: no overlap with our ~650 addresses (they're fresh, so this was expected).
Not claimed
  • Who operates naswap or naswap.io. We name no person.
  • That THORChain, its node operators or any other interface acted knowingly.
  • Formal attribution. Kelp DAO (LayerZero) and AFX (AFX / Mandiant-tracked) attributions are theirs; Bitget's is Bitget's and TRM's. We show shared infrastructure.
  • That the power-of-two seed inputs prove a specific mixer. They fit a CoinJoin denomination pattern.
